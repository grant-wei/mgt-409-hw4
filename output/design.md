# Problem 10: Campus Customs storefront design

## Direction

I used Yale Bulldog Blue's merchandise-first browsing and clear shop categories as visual reference, then gave Campus Customs its own compact storefront treatment. The design uses the course catalogue's product photography and does not reuse the reference site's assets or page layout.

## What changed and why it helps

- **Type and color:** Added DM Sans for labels and product information, with DM Serif Display for large headings. Deep Yale blue, warm off-white, and a restrained brass accent give the shop a recognizable campus feel while keeping prices and controls easy to scan.
- **Home:** Turned the existing sentence into the main headline, paired it with a featured catalogue product, a direct shop link, and a small collection preview. This puts the shop and real products in view immediately. The Home sentence remains exactly: “Yale gear for people who like Yale.”
- **Navigation:** Added a compact CC mark, clearer active-page indication, and a high-contrast account action. This makes the main paths easier to find while browsing.
- **Products:** Refined image tiles, card spacing, price emphasis, hover states, and the filter panel. Product photos with dark or light source backgrounds are blended to fit the tile. The existing search, filters, recently viewed items, and full detail links remain available.
- **Product details:** Gave the product image more space, strengthened the price and title hierarchy, and made color and size stock easier to scan. The detail page remains linked from catalogue and chat cards.
- **Chat and account pages:** Matched the floating chat panel, result cards, comparison panel, and account form to the storefront palette, with stronger spacing and focus states. Chat history now formats simple bold prices and list lines for easier scanning.
- **Motion and mobile:** Added short page, image, and chat transitions, with reduced-motion support. Narrow layouts stack the hero and product details, keep navigation horizontally usable, and constrain the chat panel to the mobile viewport.

## Visual reference

[Yale Bulldog Blue](https://yalebulldogblue.com/) informed the campus-merchandise context and category-forward shopping pattern. Campus Customs uses its own type, palette, composition, and local catalogue images.
